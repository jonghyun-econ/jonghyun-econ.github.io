My research is in **microeconomic theory**, with a focus on **information economics, learning, and networks**.

## Job Market Paper

### <a href="{{ '/assets/pdf/Recursive Bayesian Compression and False Certainty.pdf' | relative_url }}" target="_blank">Recursive Bayesian Compression and False Certainty</a>

**Abstract:** This paper studies how recursively compressing beliefs into marginal assessments can distort Bayesian learning, even when the learner observes every signal and knows the correct statistical model. I characterize which false states can become self-reinforcing under this form of belief compression, show that false certainty can arise robustly from interior beliefs, and identify exactly when marginal records contain enough information to reproduce full Bayesian updating.
